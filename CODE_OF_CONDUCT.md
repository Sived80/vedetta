# Code of Conduct

Vedetta is open to everyone who wants to understand, use or improve it, whatever their experience.

## What is expected

- Be kind and patient, especially with people who are new to Home Assistant or networking.
- Assume good intent, and explain with facts: what happened, what was expected.
- Criticise the work, not the person.
- Respect privacy: never post other people's addresses, names or exports in the clear.

## What is not accepted

- Insults, harassment, discrimination or personal attacks.
- Spam and promotion that has nothing to do with the project.
- Publishing private information of anyone without their consent.

## What happens

Comments and issues that break these rules can be edited, hidden, locked or removed, and people who repeat them can be blocked from the repository. To report a problem, use GitHub's **Report content** on the comment, or the private route described in [Security](SECURITY.md) for anything serious.

This code applies to the issues, discussions, pull requests and every other space of the project.
